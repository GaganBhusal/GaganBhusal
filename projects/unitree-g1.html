Day 3 Status: Building an end-to-end whole-body motion imitation pipeline for the 23-DOF Unitree G1 humanoid. Video pose recovery (GVHMR) and kinematic retargeting (GMR) are complete; currently training whole-body tracking via Asymmetric Actor-Critic PPO in Genesis and Mjlab, with sim-to-real transfer pending.

Project Overview & Motivation

Developing an end-to-end whole-body motion imitation pipeline for the 23-DOF Unitree G1 humanoid robot. Instead of manual trajectory tracking or hand-tuned keyframing, this system learns human-like walking and dynamic motions directly from monocular video demonstrations.

Current Status & What Works Today (Day 3)

  • What Works Today: Monocular 3D human pose recovery via GVHMR and 23-DOF kinematic retargeting via GMR are functional and verified in Genesis and Mjlab simulators for dynamic motions (including turns, kicks, and agile movements).
  • In Progress: Training whole-body imitation policy using Asymmetric Actor-Critic (PPO) — the critic accesses privileged full simulation state (ground truth contacts, forces, and obstacle geometry) while the actor policy relies strictly on proprioceptive robot states and IMU readings.
  • Next Steps / Pending: Comprehensive domain randomization (friction, joint damping, sensor noise), push disturbance recovery, and sim-to-real physical hardware transfer.

System Architecture & Methodology

The pipeline consists of three core stages:
  1. Human Pose Extraction: Extract 3D human motion sequences from monocular video demonstrations using GVHMR.
  2. Kinematic Retargeting: Map human joint trajectories to the kinematic tree, range of motion, and joint limits of the Unitree G1 via GMR.
  3. Asymmetric Actor-Critic RL: Train an asymmetric actor-critic policy with PPO in Genesis and Mjlab simulators using motion-matching and balance rewards.

Simulation Progress & Sim-to-Real Roadmap

Demonstrated accurate retargeting and simulation of dynamic human motions (including complex turns and kicks) in both Genesis and Mjlab simulators. Active policy training is focused on ground contact stability and balance before physical transfer.

Technical Specifications & Stack

  • Platform: Unitree G1 Humanoid Robot (23-DOF)
  • Status: In Progress
  • Pose Estimation: GVHMR (General Video Human Motion Recovery)
  • Kinematic Retargeting: GMR (Geometric Motion Retargeting)
  • Policy Training: Genesis Simulator & Mjlab + Asymmetric PPO
