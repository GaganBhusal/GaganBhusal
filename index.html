Unitree Go2 Sim-to-Real Quadruped Locomotion

2025–2026
SIM-TO-REAL DEMO

Trained an asymmetric actor-critic locomotion policy across 4,096 parallel environments in Genesis simulator with aggressive domain randomization. Transferred zero-shot to the physical Unitree Go2 via low-level CycloneDDS, validating stable standing, push recovery, and blind omnidirectional walking.

500 Hz Low-Level Loop
51.7 s Cont. Walk (No Falls)
4,096 Genesis Envs
0.15 rad/s Yaw RMSE

Unitree G1 Humanoid Motion Imitation

2026 · Active
GENESIS SIMULATION

Building a whole-body motion imitation pipeline for the 23-DOF Unitree G1 humanoid robot. Converts human video into robot joint targets via GVHMR and GMR retargeting, trains an expert PPO teacher in Genesis, and distills into a robust student policy for sim-to-real transfer.

PyTorch implementation of DeepMind's AlphaZero algorithm for two-player Connect-4. Uses Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS), a residual policy value neural network, and self-play data generation without human domain knowledge. Outperformed depth-10 Minimax agents.
